## ⬇️ Download saha install

| Phone eka | Download |
|---|---|
| Bohoma phones (aluth) | **[GarageBill.apk](../../releases/latest/download/GarageBill.apk)** |
| Parana phone ekaka "App not installed" awoth | **[GarageBill-old-phones.apk](../../releases/latest/download/GarageBill-old-phones.apk)** |

Hama welema **aluthma version eka** me links walin enawa.

1. Uda link eka phone eke **Chrome** eken open karanna. APK eka download wenawa.
2. Download eka open karala **Install** obanna.
3. "Install unknown apps" kiyala ahuwoth **Allow** karanna.
4. App eka kalinma thiyenawa nam **Update** wenawa. Kalin hadapu bills makenne naha.

> [!NOTE]
> "App not installed" kiyala awoth, kalin thibba app eka wena thanakin (test version ekak) awa ekak wenna puluwan.
> Ehema nam mulinma app eke **⚙️ Settings → Backup** eken backup ekak aragena, parana app eka uninstall karala, aluth eka install karala, backup eka **Restore** karanna.

## ✨ App eken karanna puluwan dewal

### 🎤 Katha karala bill hadanna
- **"New bill (the app asks)"** obama app eka ekin eka ahanawa: nama, bike number, wada, ganan
- **Sinhala**, **English** nathnam dekama kalawam katha karanna puluwan
- **Ape Sinhala Ahana AI eka**: Google nathuwa, internet nathuwa Sinhala therum gannawa
- **Bill AI eka**: ahuna deyin nama, phone, bike number, services, ganan wen karanawa
- Sadde thiyena thanakata **Call mode**, saha **Bluetooth headset mic** eka use karanna puluwan

### ✍️ Type karala bill hadanna
- 🔍 Service / part eka **search karala tap karanna**. Price list eke saha stock eke ganan auto ma enawa
- 📷 Bike number plate eke **photo ekak** gaththama number eka auto ma kiyawanawa
- 👤 Kalin aapu bike ekak nam **kalin aawa dawasa, karapu wada, km** penawa
- 🏷️ Service ekakata hari mulu bill ekata hari **discount** (Rs. hari %)
- ↩️ App eka close unath, call ekak awath, type karapu bill eka **makenne naha**

### 💵 Advance salli
- Bike eka denakotama **advance eka save karanna**. Services passe danna puluwan
- Bike eka hadala bill eka hadanakota bike number eka type karama **advance eka auto ma wetenawa**
- Bill eke **Total → Advance → To pay** kiyala penawa
- Thawa bill nokarapu advances list ekak penawa, aapahu denna **Give back** button ekath thiyenawa

### 🔧 Km saha ilanga service eka
- Service / oil change ekakata **Km eka** saha **ilanga service eka keeyada** (udaharanayak 2,500 km) danna
- Bill eke **"Next service at: 17,500 km"** kiyala penawa
- Saakshiyak widiyata **km meter eke photo ekak** ganna puluwan

### 🧾 Bill eka yawanna
- 💬 **WhatsApp** message ekak widiyata
- 📄 Lassana **PDF** bill ekak (garage name, logo, phone ekka)
- 🖨️ Bluetooth **thermal printer** (58mm / 80mm)
- ✏️ Save karapu bill ekak **wenas karanna**, waradi bill ekak **delete** karanna (**Undo** ekath ekka)

### 💰 Ganan saha report
- 📊 **Today's earnings**: ada aadayama, iye dawasata wada wedi da adu da, **dawas 7 ka chart** eka, average bill, me masaya
- 📅 **Masika report**: bills, total, parts cost, **profit**, wadiyenma aawa services, dawasen dawasata
- 💰 Garage eke **price list** eka

### 📦 Stock
- Parts walata gaththa ganna, wikunana ganna, thiyena ganana
- Bill ekata part ekak dāma **stock eken auto ma adu wenawa**, bill eka delete kalama aye ekathu wenawa
- Badu iwara wenna yanakota **rathu paatin anaturu adawiya**
- Stock eke mulu watinakama

### 🔒 Data araksha karanna
- 📴 Sampurnayenma **offline**. Bills okkoma phone eke
- ☁️ **Google Drive backup**: bill ekak save karana hama welema auto ma backup wenawa
- 💾 Backup eka file ekak widiyata save karala, aluth phone ekaka **Restore** karanna puluwan
